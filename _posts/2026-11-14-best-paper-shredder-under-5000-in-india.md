## What P2, P3 and P4 actually mean

Shredder listings borrow security levels from the German standard DIN 66399, now also published as ISO/IEC 21964. For paper, the levels that matter at this price are:

| Level | Limit for paper | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P-2 | Strips up to 6 mm wide, or particles up to 800 mm² | Internal papers, notices |
| P-3 | Strips up to 2 mm wide, or particles up to 320 mm² | Sensitive and personal data |
| P-4 | Particles up to 160 mm², strip width up to 6 mm | Particularly sensitive data such as salary statements and personal records |

Shredder maker HSM says a P-2 machine cuts an A4 page into about 35 strips, while P-4 turns it into about 389 particles. That gap is why we left strip-cut models out of the main list. For bank and tax papers, aim for P-4.

**The P4 label trap.** You can check a listing's claim yourself by multiplying the two cut dimensions. The STöK ST-30CC, for example, is sold as "P4 High Security", but its own listing says it cuts paper into 5 x 38 mm pieces. That is 190 mm², which is above the 160 mm² P-4 limit and fits P-3 instead. It is still a decent cross-cut shredder at about ₹3,759, but it is not what the label says. All four of our picks come in at or under 160 mm².

## What to shred, and what to keep first

A shredder is a money tool, not just an office gadget. UIDAI's Aadhaar usage advice (PIB, 30 December 2022) asks people not to share their Aadhaar openly in the public domain, especially on social media, and UIDAI has said residents should treat it with the same care as a bank passbook, PAN or passport. Paper copies lying in a household bin deserve the same care.

Good candidates for the shredder:

- Self-attested photocopies of Aadhaar, PAN and passport left over from KYC, rent agreements or job applications
- Cancelled cheques and old cheque books from closed accounts
- Credit card statements, expired debit and credit cards
- Old salary slips, insurance renewal notices and pre-approved loan offers

Before you shred tax papers, check how long you need them. Keep ITR acknowledgements, Form 16, Form 26AS/AIS downloads and investment proofs until any reassessment window for that year is clearly over, and ask your CA if you are unsure. When in doubt, scan first and shred later. **For small businesses:** the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025, and most duties for data fiduciaries, including security safeguards, take effect after an 18-month transition, around 13 May 2027. The Act covers digital personal data, including paper records once they are digitised. A shredder will not make you compliant on its own, but disposing of printed customer KYC copies properly is a sensible part of the same habit.

## How to choose a paper shredder under ₹5,000

1. **Cut type:** pick cross cut over strip cut for anything with account numbers.
2. **Check the maths:** width x length should be 160 mm² or less if you want true P-4.
3. **Duty cycle:** 5 to 7.5 minutes of run time is normal at this price. Feed small batches and let it cool.
4. **Sheet count is for 80 GSM paper:** stapled bank statements or thick letterheads count as more sheets. Feed one or two fewer than the rated number to avoid jams.
5. **Warranty type:** off-site means courier or carry-in, so keep the box and invoice.

## FAQ

### Which is the best paper shredder under Rs 5,000 in India?
The STöK ST-15CC at about ₹2,819 on 14 November 2026. It cuts 6 sheets at a time into 4 x 32 mm cross-cut pieces, which fits the P-4 security level, and has 4.1 stars from 1,365 Amazon ratings. The Amazon Basics 6-Sheet Cross Cut is a close alternative with a longer 7.5-minute run time.

### Is strip cut good enough for bank statements?
Not really. Strip cut usually rates P-2, which turns an A4 page into about 35 long strips that can be pieced back together. Cross cut at P-4 produces about 389 small particles per page, so pick cross cut for bank, tax and ID papers.

### How do I check if a shredder is really P4?
Multiply the two cut dimensions on the listing. P-4 under DIN 66399 allows particles up to 160 mm². A 4 x 32 mm cut is 128 mm² and qualifies; a 5 x 38 mm cut is 190 mm² and only fits P-3, even if the listing says P4.

### Can these shredders destroy credit cards and staples?
Yes. All four picks list credit card shredding, and both STöK models also list staples and paper clips. Feed one card at a time and remove binder clips and thick bulldog clips first.

### How long can a home shredder run at once?
Between about 5 and 7.5 minutes for the models in this guide. The STöK ST-15CC then needs 40 minutes to cool, and the Amazon Basics 6-Sheet Cross Cut needs 19 minutes.
