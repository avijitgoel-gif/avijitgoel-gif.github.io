## How to choose a hair dryer under ₹1,000 in India

### Wattage

- Short or fine hair: 1000 W is enough (Philips HP8100).
- Medium to long hair: 1100 to 1200 W dries faster (Havells HD3151, Philips HP8120, Nova NHP 8100).
- Very thick or curly hair: consider crossing ₹1,000 for 1400 W or more and a diffuser.

### Cool shot

A cool shot (or cool mode) lets you lock a style after drying. Havells HD3151 and AGARO Premium list one. The Philips HP8100 does not.

### Foldable handle and weight

Foldable models (Havells HD3151, Philips HP8120, Nova, Vega, AGARO) pack easier for travel. Lighter bodies are easier to hold above your head for a full dry.

### Warranty and brand service

Philips and Havells are easiest to service in most Indian cities. Prefer a clear 2-year line when two dryers look similar.

### How to dry without cooking your hair

The American Academy of Dermatology advises low or medium heat and not rubbing wet hair roughly with a towel. A 2011 *Annals of Dermatology* study found less surface damage when hair was dried from about 15 cm with the dryer kept moving. Blot first, keep the nozzle moving, and finish on cool if your dryer has it.
