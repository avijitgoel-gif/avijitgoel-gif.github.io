## Where each one wins

**Price and warranty.** Healthgenie is ₹390 cheaper and covers you for a year longer, as long as you register for the extended warranty as the box explains.

**Display and origin.** The Hoffen has a backlit display, which is easier to read in a dim bathroom, and lists India as the country of origin. It also has the biggest rating pool of the two, at over 75,000.

**Accuracy tips that apply to both.** Put the scale on a hard, flat floor, tap it with one foot, wait for 0.0, then step on. Both brands suggest ignoring the first reading after the scale has been moved. Healthgenie's listing also says it is for home use only, not for trade or medical diagnosis.
