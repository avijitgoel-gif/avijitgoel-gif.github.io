Since UIDAI retired L0 fingerprint devices (the sunset for existing L0 units was 30 June 2025), CSC operators, banking agents and families filing life certificates have had to buy an L1 scanner. The two names that come up most are Mantra and Morpho. Both are on UIDAI's L1 whitelist, so either will work for Aadhaar authentication. Here is how they differ.

## Quick verdict

- **Pick the Mantra MFS110 L1** for most uses. It is cheaper, sharper, rated better and was the number 1 seller in Amazon.in's Biometrics category.
- **Pick the Morpho MSO 1300 E3 RD L1** only if your bank, distributor or AePS app supports Morpho better.

## Where each one wins

**Price and ratings.** The Mantra is about ₹850 cheaper and has six times as many ratings. The Morpho listing had 30% one-star ratings, many about installation.

**Specs.** Mantra's datasheet lists a 500 dpi optical sensor, IP54 dust and splash rating, 9H scratch-resistant surface and capture in under 2 seconds. The Morpho is 400 dpi.

**Phone use.** The Morpho listing includes an OTG connector, which helps if you work from an Android phone. With the Mantra, buy an OTG adapter if your phone has only Type-C.

**Running cost.** Mantra charges ₹236 including GST for each extra year of L1 RD service on its official portal. "Error 1001" on a Mantra usually means the RD subscription has expired.
