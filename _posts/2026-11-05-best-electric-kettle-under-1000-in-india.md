## How to choose an electric kettle in India

### Capacity

- 1 to 2 people: 1.2 to 1.5 litres
- 3 to 4 people: 1.5 litres
- 5 or more people, or frequent guests: 1.8 litres

### Wattage and boiling time

Higher wattage boils faster. As a rough physics estimate, heating 1.5 litres of water from 25°C to 100°C needs about 471 kJ of energy. A 1500 W kettle supplies that in about 5.2 minutes if no heat is lost, and a 1300 W kettle in about 6 minutes. Real-world times are a little longer because some heat escapes.

### Running cost

A 1500 W kettle running for 5 minutes uses about 0.125 kWh. At an electricity tariff of ₹8 per unit, that is about ₹1 per full boil. Wattage barely changes the energy used per boil; it mainly changes how long you wait.

### Safety features to insist on

- **Automatic shut-off:** every kettle in our list has it.
- **Boil-dry protection:** cuts power if the kettle is switched on empty.
- **Cool-touch handle and lid:** important in homes with children.
- **ISI mark:** look for it on the product or listing, as on the Prestige PKOSS and Lifelong LLEK15.

### Cleaning

A wide mouth, as on the Milton Rapid, lets you reach in to clean. In hard-water cities, boil a solution of water and a little vinegar or lemon juice every few weeks to remove scale, then rinse well.
