## How to choose: four questions before you buy

### 1. Do you run more than one shift?

This is the question most buyers skip. The ZKTeco LX50 listing says plainly that it "can not be set night shift, only one shift is available". That is fine for a 10 to 7 office. It is a problem for a pharmacy, a hostel kitchen or a small factory where people clock in at 10 pm and out at 6 am, because the report will split one shift across two dates.

Hikvision's datasheet for the DS-K1A802AEF lists up to 32 normal shifts, 32 man-hour shifts and 32 holiday schedules. If you have rotating shifts, that is the safest pick in this budget.

### 2. Fingerprint only, or fingerprint plus card?

Fingerprints fail on some people: construction workers, cooks and older staff often have worn ridges. A card or PIN fallback saves a lot of arguments. The Team Office Z100N, Hikvision and Realtime C101+ all read RFID cards. The CP PLUS datasheet on cpplusworld.com lists card capacity of 1,000, but the Amazon title only mentions fingerprint and password, so confirm with the seller if cards matter to you. The ZKTeco LX50 is fingerprint and PIN only.

### 3. USB pen drive or network?

Every machine here can export reports to a USB pen drive, which is how most small offices work: once a month someone plugs in a drive and opens the Excel file. ZKTeco's Amazon listing asks for a drive of up to 8 GB formatted FAT32, so an old small drive is ideal. Our [best pen drive under ₹1,000 guide](https://avijitgoel-gif.github.io/gadget-guides-india/best-pen-drive-under-1000-in-india/) explains FAT32 and its limits.

Only the Hikvision has a LAN port and Wi-Fi in this budget, so you can pull logs to a PC without walking to the wall. If your office Wi-Fi does not reach the entrance, see our [Wi-Fi range extender guide](https://avijitgoel-gif.github.io/gadget-guides-india/best-wifi-range-extender-under-3000-in-india/).

### 4. What happens in a power cut?

The ZKTeco listing says "it doesn't have battery, works with 5v adapter only", and Hikvision's battery version is the separate "-B" model, not the one sold here. Nobody can punch until power returns. If your area has frequent cuts, plug the adapter into the same backup as your router or PC. Our [UPS for computer guide](/best-ups-for-computer-under-5000-in-india/) covers small 600 VA units.

## Your staff fingerprints and the DPDP Act

A fingerprint template is personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Here is what an employer should know, based on the Act and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified on 13 November 2025, with the commencement notification G.S.R. 843(E)):

- **Timing.** Most duties for businesses, including notice, security safeguards and breach reporting, come into force on 13 May 2027, eighteen months after notification.
- **Consent.** Section 7(i) lets an employer process personal data "for the purposes of employment" without separate consent. Attendance is a natural fit, but keep the data to that purpose.
- **Security.** Section 8(5) requires reasonable security safeguards. Rule 6 of the DPDP Rules lists measures such as access control, logging and backups. In practice: keep the admin PIN private, do not leave exported reports on shared pen drives, and delete enrolled fingerprints when someone leaves.
- **Penalty.** The Schedule to the Act allows a penalty of up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards. Small firms are unlikely targets, but it shows how seriously the law treats leaks.

A standalone USB machine keeps templates inside the device, which limits exposure. Cloud models add convenience but also a third party processor, so read their data terms.

## Setting up in 15 minutes

1. Mount the machine at chest height near the entrance, away from direct sunlight, which can affect optical sensors.
2. Set date and time first. Wrong clocks are the most common reason for bad reports.
3. Enrol two fingers per person, so a cut finger does not lock anyone out.
4. Create one admin and note the admin PIN somewhere safe. Resetting a locked machine usually needs the seller.
5. Export a test report on day one and open it on your PC before you rely on it for salary.
