If you are opening your first demat account, the three names you hear most are Zerodha, Upstox and Groww. All three let you open an account online for free. The real difference shows up later: in the yearly maintenance fee (AMC), the brokerage you pay per order, and the DP charge every time you sell shares from your demat.

This guide puts those numbers side by side so you can pick based on how you actually plan to invest. All figures below come from the brokers' own pricing pages and independent comparisons published in September and October 2026. Charges change, so confirm on the broker's site before you sign up.

## Quick answer

- **You mostly buy and hold stocks for the long term:** Zerodha charges zero brokerage on equity delivery, which is hard to beat for this use.
- **You want no yearly AMC at all:** Groww lists ₹0 demat AMC.
- **You already like the Upstox app and trade F&O:** Upstox options are a flat ₹20 per executed order, the same as the other two, and its first year of AMC is free.

Most of that total is statutory charges that you pay anywhere. The gap of about ₹55 per round trip comes from Zerodha's zero delivery brokerage. If you make only a few long-term trades a year, the gap is small. If you buy and sell delivery positions every week, it adds up.

## Zerodha: best for long-term delivery investors

Zerodha's main draw is zero brokerage on equity delivery. If your plan is to buy shares or ETFs and hold them, you pay no brokerage on those orders. Intraday is the lower of 0.03% or ₹20, which is also cheaper on small intraday orders than the 0.1% rate the other two use.

The catch is the AMC. After the free first year, a regular (non-BSDA) account pays ₹300 a year plus 18% GST. Its DP charge on each sell is ₹15.34 per scrip, the lowest of the three per the comparison above.

**Pick Zerodha if:** you invest in stocks for the long term and do not mind a yearly AMC from year two.

## Upstox: flat ₹20 per order, free first year

Upstox charges ₹20 per executed order on equity delivery, and the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% on intraday. Equity options are a flat ₹20 per executed order and futures are the lower of ₹20 or 0.05%. Mutual funds and IPOs carry zero commission.

New customers get zero AMC in the first year. After that, non-BSDA users pay ₹300 + GST. Upstox notes that an account processing fee may apply during onboarding, so check the final screen before you pay anything. The DP charge is ₹20 + GST per scrip per day on sells.

**Pick Upstox if:** you trade F&O or intraday and prefer its app, and you are fine paying ₹20 on delivery orders.

## Groww: best if you want zero AMC

Groww lists ₹0 for account opening and ₹0 demat AMC. That makes it the simplest choice if you will invest small amounts and rarely sell, because you will not pay a yearly fee just to keep the account open.

Brokerage on delivery and intraday is the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% per executed order, with a ₹5 minimum. On a small ₹2,000 order, 0.1% is ₹2, so you pay the ₹5 minimum. On large orders you hit the ₹20 cap. The DP charge on sells is ₹3.5 depository plus ₹16.5 Groww, plus GST, and Groww's share is ₹0 when the debit is under ₹100.

**Pick Groww if:** you are a beginner, want mutual funds and stocks in one app, and want to avoid an AMC.

## How to choose in 3 steps

### 1. Count how often you will sell

DP charges apply on every sell from your demat, at roughly ₹15 to ₹20 plus GST per scrip. If you sell rarely, this barely matters. If you rebalance often, it matters more than the brokerage headline.

### 2. Decide if you will trade delivery or intraday

Delivery investors save the most on Zerodha. Intraday traders with small orders benefit from Zerodha's 0.03% rate. For F&O options, all three charge about ₹20 per order, so pick on app and features.

### 3. Factor in AMC over 5 years

Zerodha and Upstox are free for year one and then ₹300 + GST a year for non-BSDA accounts. Groww is ₹0. Over several years, that AMC difference can outweigh the brokerage savings if you trade very little.

## Which one should you open?

There is no single winner. Zerodha wins for active long-term delivery buyers. Groww wins for low-activity investors who hate yearly fees. Upstox sits in the middle with a familiar flat ₹20 model and a free first year. Many investors open two accounts, for example Groww for mutual funds and Zerodha for stocks, since opening is free on both.

## FAQ

### Is account opening free on Zerodha, Upstox and Groww?
Yes, all three list free online account opening for resident individuals. Upstox notes an account processing fee may be charged during onboarding.

### Which broker has zero AMC?
Groww lists ₹0 demat AMC. Zerodha and Upstox waive AMC in the first year, then charge ₹300 + GST a year for non-BSDA accounts.

### Which is cheapest for delivery trades?
Zerodha, with zero brokerage on equity delivery. On a ₹1,00,000 round trip, total charges were ₹237.82 on Zerodha against ₹293.28 on Upstox and Groww in an October 2026 calculation.

### What is a DP charge?
It is a fee charged when shares leave your demat account on a sell. It is about ₹15.34 per scrip on Zerodha and ₹20 + GST per scrip on Upstox and Groww (Groww's share is waived under ₹100 debit).

### Can I have accounts with more than one broker?
Yes. You can hold demat accounts with multiple brokers under the same PAN.
