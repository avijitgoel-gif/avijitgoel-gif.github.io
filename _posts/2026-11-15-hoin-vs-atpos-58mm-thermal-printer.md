The HOIN 58 mm and the ATPOS H-58BT are two of the most-reviewed small billing printers on Amazon.in. Both print 58 mm bills over Bluetooth or USB, both use ESC/POS commands that billing apps understand, and both carried 4.3 stars when we checked. Neither has a battery or an auto cutter.

## Quick verdict

- **Pick the HOIN** if you want the longer rating history and a clearly stated 1-year warranty.
- **Pick the ATPOS** if you want to save about ₹200 and like the built-in power supply.

## Where each one wins

**App support.** HOIN names the common Indian billing apps outright, which saves guesswork. ATPOS lists ESC/POS compatibility, which most of the same apps use, but check your app's printer list.

**Power.** ATPOS has a built-in power supply, so there is no adapter brick. It also quotes a print head life of 50 to 100 km of paper. Its listing is clear that it is not portable and has no backup, so both printers stop in a power cut unless they are on an inverter or UPS.

**Price.** ATPOS is about ₹200 cheaper. HOIN has roughly 800 more ratings behind it.

If you need to print away from a plug point, neither is right; look at a battery model such as the SEZNIK Veer instead.
