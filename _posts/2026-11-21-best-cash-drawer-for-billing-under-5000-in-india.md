## How a cash drawer opens (and why the printer matters)

A POS cash drawer has no USB port and no power cable of its own in this price band. It plugs into the receipt printer with a small phone-style RJ11 or RJ12 cable, and the printer powers it.

Epson's technical reference for its TM-T82X receipt printer explains the mechanism. The printer's drawer kick connector has six pins. Pin 4 supplies +24 V, and the printer sends a short drive pulse on pin 2 or pin 5 to fire the drawer's solenoid. Pin 3 can read a switch that tells the software whether the drawer is open. Epson says the solenoid must have a resistance of 24 ohms or more, or draw 1 A or less, and that you must use the printer's 24 V output to drive it ([Epson TM-T82X Technical Reference Guide](https://download4.epson.biz/sec_pubs/bs/pdf/TM-T82X_trg_en_revF.pdf)).

The pulse itself comes from a standard command. In Epson's ESC/POS reference, the command ESC p sends a pulse to drawer pin 2 or pin 5, with the on time and off time set in steps of 2 milliseconds ([Epson ESC/POS reference, ESC p](https://download4.epson.biz/sec_pubs/pos/reference_en/escpos/esc_lp.html)).

What this means when you buy:

1. **Your printer needs a drawer port.** Many 58 mm Bluetooth printers do not have one. Look for "cash drawer support" or an RJ11/RJ12 port on the printer's listing.
2. **Match the voltage.** The Sagrach listing says it works on 12 V or 24 V and recommends 24 V. The 24x7 eMall lists 24 V. The others do not state a voltage, so ask the seller before you buy if your printer is not a common 24 V model.
3. **Pin 2 or pin 5.** If the drawer does not open, the software may be sending the pulse to the other pin. Epson notes that which pin works depends on the drawer, so try the other setting in your billing app before you call it faulty.
4. **Never plug a phone line into it.** Epson warns that a telephone line in the drawer kick connector can damage the printer.

## A cash drawer is also a cash control tool

When the drawer is in "auto" mode, it opens only when a bill prints. Every opening leaves a printed receipt behind, so at closing you can match the cash in the drawer against the day's bills in your software. Keep the key with the owner or manager, because the manual position skips that check.

Cash limits matter too. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, which replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026, section 186 carries over the old section 269ST rule: you cannot receive ₹2,00,000 or more in cash from one person in a day, for a single transaction, or for one event. The penalty can equal the amount received ([Income-tax Act, 2025](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf); section mapping summarised by [TaxClue](https://taxclue.in/blog/section-269ss-269st-income-tax-act-2025-mapping)).

### Can I connect a cash drawer directly to my computer?
Not the drawers in this list. They have an RJ11/RJ12 port and are powered and opened by a receipt printer with a drawer kick port. The billing software tells the printer to send the opening pulse.

### Why does my cash drawer not open when a bill prints?
Check four things: the key is in the auto position, the cable is in the printer's drawer port, the billing software has cash drawer opening turned on, and the software is pulsing the right pin. Epson printers can pulse pin 2 or pin 5, and which one works depends on the drawer.

### Do 58 mm thermal printers support cash drawers?
Some do, many do not. Small Bluetooth 58 mm printers often skip the drawer port. Check the printer listing for an RJ11/RJ12 drawer port or the words cash drawer support.

### How much cash can a shop accept from one customer?
