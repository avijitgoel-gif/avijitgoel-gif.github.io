## L0 vs L1: why your old scanner stopped working

UIDAI only allows "registered devices" for Aadhaar authentication. These digitally sign every fingerprint capture so that a stored or replayed print cannot be used. There are two security levels:

- **L0 devices** did the signing and encryption in software on the host PC or phone.
- **L1 devices** do it inside the scanner, in a Trusted Execution Environment, with keys stored in secure hardware. UIDAI launched L1 fingerprint devices in 2022 and added fake finger detection to them in 2025.

UIDAI stopped whitelisting new L0 fingerprint devices from 1 October 2024. Its March 2025 circular extended the sunset for existing L0 devices to 30 June 2025 and listed the hotlisting dates for each model. Two of the most common names on that list are the **Mantra MFS100** and the **Access FM220U** (non-L1), both hotlisted from 1 June 2025. UIDAI also warned that an extra fee may be charged on transactions done with L0 devices from 1 July 2025.

So the first rule is simple. On Amazon, look for "L1" in the model name itself: MFS110 L1, FM220U L1 or MSO 1300 E3 RD L1. Listings that say only "MFS100" or "FM220" are the old generation, whatever the title claims about "latest RD service".

## Do pensioners even need a scanner for the life certificate?

Often not. The Department of Pension and Pensioners' Welfare is running its fifth nationwide Digital Life Certificate campaign from 1 to 30 November 2026. Pensioners aged 80 and above could submit from 1 October, and everyone else from 1 November.

The department is pushing **face authentication**: install the Jeevan Pramaan Face app and the AadhaarFaceRD app on an Android or iOS phone, enter the Aadhaar, PPO and pension account details, and complete a face scan. That costs nothing. Bank branches, CSCs, post offices and doorstep services are other options.

A fingerprint scanner makes sense if you file for several family members or neighbours every year, if face authentication keeps failing, or if you run a CSC or banking point where fingerprint capture is part of daily work.

## FAQ

### Which is the best L1 fingerprint scanner for Aadhaar in India?
The Mantra MFS110 L1 for most people. It is UIDAI-whitelisted with a device certificate valid to October 2028, was the number 1 seller in Amazon.in's Biometrics category in November 2026 and cost about ₹2,485.

### Can I still use a Mantra MFS100 for Aadhaar authentication?
No. The MFS100 is an L0 device. UIDAI hotlisted it from 1 June 2025, and all L0 fingerprint devices reached their sunset on 30 June 2025. You need an L1 model such as the MFS110 L1.

### What is RD service and how much does renewal cost?
RD (registered device) service is the driver and certificate that lets a scanner sign fingerprint captures for UIDAI. Most new scanners include one year. For the Mantra MFS110 L1, one more year on Mantra's official portal costs ₹236 including GST.

### What is the difference between L0 and L1 devices?
L0 devices encrypted and signed fingerprint data in software on the host computer. L1 devices do it inside the scanner in a Trusted Execution Environment with keys in secure hardware, which UIDAI says gives stronger protection against spoofing.

### Do I need a fingerprint scanner to submit a pensioner's life certificate?
Not necessarily. Pensioners can generate a Digital Life Certificate free with face authentication using the Jeevan Pramaan Face and AadhaarFaceRD apps on a smartphone, or visit a bank, CSC or post office.
