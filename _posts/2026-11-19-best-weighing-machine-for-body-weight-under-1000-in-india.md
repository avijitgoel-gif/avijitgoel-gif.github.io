## 50 g or 100 g: what graduation means

The graduation is the step size of the display. A 100 g scale like the Hoffen HO-18 shows 64.3 kg, then 64.4 kg. A 50 g scale like the ANT Flora Bud can also show 64.35 kg.

That finer step looks better on paper, but your body weight moves by far more than 50 g over a day because of food, water and the bathroom. For tracking a trend over weeks, 100 g is enough. What matters more is that the scale gives the same reading when you step on it twice in a row. Several listings, including Healthgenie, Hoffen and Boldfit, tell you to "initialise" the scale after moving it: tap it with one foot, wait for it to return to 0.0, then step on. Hoffen and Healthgenie also suggest ignoring the first reading.

## Does the body fat feature work?

Smart scales estimate body fat with bioelectrical impedance: a tiny current passes through your bare feet, and the scale uses an equation to guess fat and muscle. Harvard Health says the accuracy and relevance of these readings "isn't well established", and that people with a pacemaker or defibrillator should not use the feature ([Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/heart-health/device-of-the-month-body-weight-scale)).

Hydration changes the result. Harvard's Nutrition Source notes the body water to fat ratio shifts with illness, dehydration or weight loss, which lowers accuracy, and that readings may be less accurate at a BMI of 35 or higher ([Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/healthy-weight/measuring-fat/)). Consumer Reports found that smart scales were not more accurate than plain digital ones for weight, and in a 2016 test the body fat readings of consumer scales were off by 21 to 34 percent against a professional device ([Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/health/bathroom-scales/buying-guide/)).

**What this means:** buy the BS161 for app logging, and treat its body fat number as a trend, not a diagnosis. If you only want weight, a ₹400 scale does the same job.

## Your home scale is not legal for trade

The Healthgenie listing carries a blunt disclaimer: personal home use only, not for trade, commercial transactions or medical diagnosis. There is a legal reason.

Under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, any weight or measure used in a transaction, or "for protection", must be verified before use. "Protection" covers using a reading to decide a step needed to safeguard the well-being of a human being ([Legal Metrology Act, Section 2(k)](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/144905788/); [Section 24](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/122504373/)). The Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011 then require such instruments to be re-verified and stamped at fixed intervals ([Rule 27](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/19271422/)). Delhi's Weights and Measures Department adds that no one may use an unverified, unstamped weight for commercial purposes ([Delhi W&M FAQ](https://weightnmeasures.delhi.gov.in/faqs?page=1)).

So if you run a gym, a clinic, a diet consultancy or a shop and you charge for weighing people or goods, these ₹400 scales are the wrong tool. Buy a verified and stamped scale from a licensed dealer. ## How to get a consistent reading

- **Hard, flat floor.** Every listing asks for tile or stone, not a rug or mat.
- **Initialise after moving it.** Tap with one foot, wait for 0.0, then step on.
- **Same time, same conditions.** Hoffen suggests early morning on an empty stomach. Weigh in similar clothes each time.
- **Stand still, feet centred.** Leaning shifts the load across the sensors.
- **Replace batteries at the "Lo" warning.** Weak cells can cause odd readings.

### Is a 50 g or 100 g weighing scale better?
A 50 g scale shows smaller steps, but body weight changes by much more than 50 g during a day. For tracking weight over weeks, a 100 g scale is enough. Consistency between repeat readings matters more than the display step.

### Are body fat scales accurate?
Not for exact numbers. Harvard Health says the accuracy of bioelectrical impedance readings is not well established, and Consumer Reports found consumer body fat readings off by 21 to 34 percent in a 2016 test. Use them to watch trends under the same conditions.

### Can I use a home weighing machine in my clinic or shop?
No. Under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, weights used in a transaction or to decide steps for a person's well-being must be verified and stamped. Home scales like these are sold for personal use only.

### Why does my digital scale show a different weight each time?
Usually because it was moved and not reset, it is on an uneven or soft floor, or the batteries are low. Place it on a hard floor, tap it until it shows 0.0, then step on and stand still.
