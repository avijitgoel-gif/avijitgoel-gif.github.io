## The stamp matters more than the model

Section 24 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 requires any scale used to sell by weight to be verified before use, and Section 33 fines the use of an unverified scale ₹2,000 to ₹10,000 ([Legal Metrology Act, 2009](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/4892/1/legalmetrology_act_2009.pdf)). Electronic scales must then be re-verified every 12 months under Rule 27 of the Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011 ([Rule 27](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/19271422/)). Rule 27(1) ties verification to the state where the scale is used, so if your shop is outside Uttar Pradesh, ask your district Legal Metrology office whether the Kanpur certificate is accepted.
