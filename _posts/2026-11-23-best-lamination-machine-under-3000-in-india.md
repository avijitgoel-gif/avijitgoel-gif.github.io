## A4 or A3: pick by your biggest document

- **Pick A4** if you laminate documents, photos and cards. It is cheaper, lighter (about 0.8 to 1.2 kg here) and takes less desk space.
- **Pick A3** if you run a shop, school or clinic and need menus, notice boards, rate charts or posters. The Amazon Basics A303 is about ₹500 more than the A401 and also adds cold mode.

One detail people miss: a pouch is a little wider than the paper inside it, because it needs a sealed border. That is why A4 machines usually list a 9 inch entry width rather than exactly 21 cm. Keep that in mind when you read the STöK LA4 notes below.

## What 80 micron and 125 micron actually mean

Pouch thickness is the most confusing part of buying a laminator, and the listings do not explain it.

Fellowes, one of the biggest pouch makers, says its pouches are described by thickness per side, so an 80 micron pouch is 80 microns on each side and 160 microns in total ([Fellowes pouch guide](https://www.fellowes.com/au/en/catalog/business-machines/resources/pg/choose-the-right-laminating-pouch)). Leitz says the same thing in its A3 laminator manual: a 125 micron pouch is 250 microns thick in total, but you should still run it on the machine's 125 micron setting ([Leitz Office PRO A3 manual](https://www.leitz.com/product/document/113482/pdf)).

So when the STöK L11A and LA3 say "80 to 125 micron", they mean the common 80 and 125 micron pouches sold in India, not 250 micron board-like pouches. Dahle's guidance is a good rule of thumb: 80 micron for documents and photos, 125 micron for things handled a lot, such as signs, ID cards and menus ([Dahle FAQ](https://www.novus-dahle.com/en/faqs/faq/laminating-pouches-thickness/)).

If a pouch box says "250 micron" in India, check whether that means 2 x 125 or 250 per side before you feed it to a budget machine.

## Hot and cold lamination, and what never to laminate

Hot lamination melts the glue layer inside the pouch with heated rollers. Cold mode just runs the rollers without heat, for self-adhesive cold pouches, and is the safe choice for heat sensitive items. The Amazon Basics A401 is hot only. The A303, STöK L11A and STöK LA3 list both modes.

Laminator manuals are clear on a few things to avoid:

- **Thermal paper.** Many shop bills and ATM slips are printed on thermal paper. Ledco's manual warns that heat lamination will turn thermal paper black ([Ledco HRD20 manual](https://www.ledcolaminator.com/images/pdf/HRD20OperationManual.pdf)). Photocopy or scan the receipt and laminate the copy.
- **Staples and pins.** The same manual says paper clips and staples can damage the rubber rollers.
- **One-of-a-kind or valuable items.** Ledco advises against laminating stamps, autographs and collectibles, because lamination can destroy their value.
- **Cards with a magnetic strip.** 3M's Scotch TL901X manual lists credit cards, cards with a magnetic strip, currency, stamps and thermal papers as items not meant for the laminator ([3M Scotch TL901X manual](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1951377O/scotch-thermal-laminator-tl901x.pdf)). The same manual says laminated items get hot and the machine should not be left on unattended.

### Should you laminate your Aadhaar?

You do not need to. UIDAI says all forms of Aadhaar, the letter, the PVC card, e-Aadhaar and mAadhaar, are equally valid ([UIDAI, Your Aadhaar](https://uidai.gov.in/en/your-aadhaar)). In 2018 UIDAI's then CEO said there is no need to get a paper Aadhaar laminated, and that a printout of a downloaded Aadhaar on ordinary paper is valid ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/wealth/personal-finance-news/plastic-or-pvc-aadhaar-smart-card-is-not-usable-uidai/articleshow/62802670.cms)). If you want a durable card, order the official Aadhaar PVC card from UIDAI instead of handing your details to a shop.

## How to choose: a quick checklist

1. **Biggest document.** A4 for papers and photos, A3 for menus and charts.
2. **Cold mode.** Get it if you laminate photos with heat sensitive inks or use cold pouches. The A401 does not have it.
3. **Pouch range.** Stick to 80 or 125 micron (per side) pouches unless the machine says otherwise.
4. **Warranty.** All five list 1 year. STöK's is offsite, so keep the box and invoice.

## FAQ

### Which is the best lamination machine under Rs 3,000 in India?
The STöK L11A at about Rs 1,691 on 23 November 2026. It takes A4 and smaller in hot or cold mode, handles 80 to 125 micron pouches, and has 4.2 stars from 3,508 ratings on Amazon.in. For A3, the Amazon Basics A303 at about Rs 1,799 is the cheapest well-rated option.

### What does 125 micron mean on a laminating pouch?
It usually means 125 microns per side, so about 250 microns in total. Fellowes and Leitz both describe pouches this way, and Leitz says a 125 micron pouch should be run on the 125 micron setting.

### Can I laminate a shop bill or ATM receipt?
Not with heat if it is printed on thermal paper, as many receipts are, and laminator manuals warn that heat lamination turns thermal paper black. Laminate a photocopy instead.

### Do I need to laminate my Aadhaar card?
No. UIDAI says the Aadhaar letter, PVC card, e-Aadhaar and mAadhaar are all equally valid. If you want a durable card, order the official PVC card from UIDAI.

### Can an A3 laminator do A4 sheets?
Yes. An A3 machine takes A3 and anything smaller, including A4 and ID card pouches.
