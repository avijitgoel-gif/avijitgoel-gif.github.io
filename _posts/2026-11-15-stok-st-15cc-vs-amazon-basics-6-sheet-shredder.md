Both shredders cost about ₹2,800, take 6 sheets per pass and cut paper into 4 x 32 mm pieces. That is 128 mm², inside the 160 mm² limit for the P-4 security level under DIN 66399. So the choice comes down to run time, bin size and how much rating history you want.

## Quick verdict

- **Pick the Amazon Basics** if you shred in longer sessions. It runs for 7.5 minutes before a 19-minute cool-down.
- **Pick the STöK ST-15CC** if you want the bigger bin, staple and paper clip shredding, and the longer review history.

## Where each one wins

**Duty cycle.** This is the biggest real difference. With the STöK, a 5-minute run is followed by a 40-minute wait. The Amazon Basics gives you 50% more run time and less than half the cool-down. If you are clearing a year of bank statements in one evening, that adds up.

**Build and extras.** The STöK is heavier at 4 kg, lists staples and paper clips as well as cards, and has a window so you can see when the bin is full. Its four-position switch includes a safety lock.

**Track record.** The STöK has 1,365 ratings to the Amazon Basics' 294, so there is more owner feedback behind it.

**Watch the listing.** Amazon Basics sells a similar 8-sheet model that runs only 3 minutes before a 30-minute cool-down, and a 6-sheet strip-cut model that is lower security. Make sure the listing says 6-sheet cross cut.
