## Rule one: buy only from an authorised seller

A hardware wallet is only as safe as its supply chain. A device that was opened and tampered with, or one that arrives with a recovery phrase already printed on a card, can be used to steal everything you put on it.

Ledger's official reseller page lists two online sellers for India: **Etherbit Private Limited** and **Amazon India (Starone Electronics)**. It also warns that the "Ledger Authorized Store" on marketplaces such as Amazon is run by a reseller, so you should check the seller name ([Ledger reseller list](https://shop.ledger.com/pages/resellers)). Ledger's own India guide says the same thing and adds that you should run the **Genuine Check** in the Ledger Wallet app during setup to confirm the Secure Element is real before moving any funds ([Ledger, Crypto in India guide](https://www.ledger.com/academy/topics/country-guides/crypto-in-india-the-ultimate-guide)).

On 26 November 2026, all three Ledger Nano S Plus listings we checked (Amethyst Purple, Gold and Matte Black) were sold by Starone Electronics and shipped from Amazon. The Matte Black listing names the importer as Luxury Personified LLP, Faridabad. Both Tangem packs were sold by TANGEM OFFICIAL INDIA and shipped from Amazon.

Two red flags, whatever the brand:

1. **A recovery phrase already in the box.** A genuine wallet always makes you create a new phrase on the device. Never use one that came printed.
2. **A seller that is not the brand's listed reseller.** We saw a Ledger Nano X "+ Billfodl" bundle with only 5 ratings and no clear seller, and an Arculus card from a third-party seller at ₹9,820.70 that is not shipped by Amazon. We left both out.

## Why self-custody matters: the WazirX lesson

On 18 July 2024, Indian exchange WazirX lost more than $230 million from one of its multisig wallets and paused all crypto and rupee withdrawals ([WazirX statement](https://wazirx.com/blog/important-update-cyber-attack-incident-and-measures-to-protect-your-assets/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/18/indias-wazirx-confirms-security-breach-after-230-million-suspicious-transfer/)). WazirX later said the theft equalled about 45% of user funds and proposed a socialised loss plan. Coins that users had already moved to wallets they controlled were not held by the exchange, so they were outside that loss.

Indian exchanges must register with the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) as reporting entities under the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, after the Finance Ministry notification S.O. 1072(E) of 7 March 2023 brought exchange, transfer and safekeeping of virtual digital assets under the Act ([Gazette notification](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2023/244184.pdf)). Registration covers anti-money-laundering duties. It is not a guarantee that your coins are safe.

## Tax: a hardware wallet does not change what you owe

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026. The rules on crypto did not change in substance, only the section numbers:

| Rule | Old section (1961 Act) | New section (2025 Act) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30% tax on income from transfer of a virtual digital asset, only cost of acquisition deductible, no loss set-off | 115BBH | 194(1), Table Sl. No. 4 |
| 1% TDS on consideration for transfer of a virtual digital asset | 194S | 393(1), Table Sl. No. 8(vi) |
| TDS threshold: Rs 50,000 a year for specified individuals and HUFs, Rs 10,000 for others | 194S(3) | 393(4), Table Sl. No. 12 |

Sources: [Income-tax Act, 2025 as amended by Finance Act, 2026](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf), [Section 393 on the Income Tax Department site](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-393-6), [Mint explainer](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/income-tax-act-2025-what-crypto-investors-should-know-about-vda-provisions-tds-and-itr-filing-rules/amp-11790360376855.html).

Practical point: keep a record of what you paid for every coin before you move it off an exchange. Once the coins sit in your own wallet, the exchange's tax statement will not track them for you.

## FAQ

### Which is the best crypto hardware wallet under Rs 10,000 in India?
For a laptop or Android user, the Ledger Nano S Plus at about Rs 5,889 on 26 November 2026, sold on Amazon.in by Ledger's authorised seller Starone Electronics. For iPhone users, the Tangem Wallet 2-card pack at about Rs 5,245, because the Nano S Plus does not work with iOS.

### Is it legal to own a crypto hardware wallet in India?
Owning a hardware wallet device is not banned, and Amazon.in sells them openly. Crypto itself is taxed as a virtual digital asset: 30% on income from transfers under section 194(1) of the Income-tax Act, 2025, plus 1% TDS under section 393(1). Crypto is not legal tender in India.

### How do I know my Ledger is genuine?
Buy from a seller on Ledger's official reseller list (for India: Etherbit or Amazon India via Starone Electronics), make sure the device asks you to create a new recovery phrase, and run Ledger's Genuine Check in the Ledger Wallet app during setup.

### Does Tangem need a seed phrase?
No. Tangem uses the second or third card in the pack as the backup. A seed phrase can be turned on as an option for advanced users, according to the Amazon.in listing.

### What happens if I lose my hardware wallet?
Your coins live on the blockchain, not on the device. With a Ledger you restore them on a new device using your secret recovery phrase. With Tangem you use a backup card. If you lose both the device and the backup, the funds cannot be recovered.
