## VA vs watts: the number that actually matters

Every UPS is sold by its VA (volt-ampere) figure, but your devices draw watts. A "600 VA" UPS on this list can only power 360 W. The ZEBRONICS U1205 is "1,000 VA" but 600 W.

APC's own advice for its Back-UPS range is simple: add up the wattage of everything you plug into the battery outlets, and if the total is more than the UPS watt rating, the UPS will not be able to support it during a power cut ([APC FAQ](https://www.apc.com/us/en/faqs/FA158811/)).

To size yours, read the wattage on the power supply label of your CPU cabinet and on the back of your monitor, or check their manuals. Then:

1. Add the watts of everything that must stay on (PC, monitor, router).
2. Keep that total well under the UPS watt rating, not the VA figure.
3. Remember that the lighter the load, the longer the backup.

## How long will the backup really last?

Listing pages are vague here. The APC listing on Amazon.in shows a runtime of "1 hour and 15 minutes", and the Microtek listing says "1 hour and 5 minutes" in one place and "15 minutes" in another. APC's official datasheet for the BX600C-IN gives a much clearer picture:

| Load on APC BX600C-IN | Runtime (APC datasheet) |
| --- | --- |
| 50 W | 40 minutes |
| 100 W | 17 minutes |
| 180 W (half load) | 4 minutes 36 seconds |
| 200 W | 3 minutes 50 seconds |
| 300 W | 1 minute |

APC notes these were measured with a new, fully charged battery. The Artis listing gives a similar real-world figure: up to 45 minutes depending on load, but 15 to 20 minutes for a PC, monitor and router together. V-Guard rates its Sesto 600 at 10 to 15 minutes on a full charge.

**What this means:** a 600 VA UPS is a "save and shut down" device, not a power backup for long cuts. If your area loses power for an hour at a time, you need an inverter for the whole room, not a bigger computer UPS. If you only need the Wi-Fi to stay up, a small router UPS is cheaper and lasts hours, which is why several of the category's best sellers are router UPS units.

## Never put a laser printer on your UPS

If your counter has a laser printer, plug it into the wall, not into the UPS. APC is blunt about this: because of the large amount of power they consume, it does not recommend protecting laser printers with a UPS, and says they should not go into a Back-UPS battery outlet or surge-only outlet ([Schneider Electric FAQ](https://www.se.com/us/en/faqs/FA158812/)). The heavy draw can make the UPS switch to battery again and again and drain it. V-Guard's Sesto 600 listing also says it supports a printer "except laser printer".

Small thermal billing printers draw far less, but check the printer's adapter rating before you plug it in. See our [thermal printer guide](/best-thermal-printer-for-billing-under-5000-in-india/) for billing printer picks.

## Check the BIS registration

UPS and inverters are on the Bureau of Indian Standards Compulsory Registration Scheme list, with registration under IS 16242 (Part 1) required since 1 March 2016 ([BIS CRS product list](https://www.crsbis.in/BIS/products-bis.do)). Look for the BIS standard mark with an R-number on the box or the label. Both APC (in its datasheet) and Artis (in its listing) state BIS certification.

## How to choose a UPS for your computer

- **Watts, not VA.** Total your devices' watts and stay well below the UPS watt rating.
- **Backup is minutes, not hours.** Plan for 5 to 15 minutes with a PC and monitor.
- **Battery warranty.** The battery is the part that wears out. Two years beats one.
- **Input voltage range.** A wider range (like 140 V to 300 V) means fewer switches to battery in areas with unstable voltage.
- **Router only?** Buy a router UPS instead, and avoid models that cut off below 40 W.
- **Pair it with a stabiliser for other gear.** A UPS protects the PC; for a TV, see our [voltage stabilizer guide](/best-voltage-stabilizer-for-tv-under-2500-in-india/).

### How much backup does a 600 VA UPS give?
Only a few minutes with a desktop PC. APC's datasheet for the BX600C-IN lists 40 minutes at 50 W, 17 minutes at 100 W and under 4 minutes at 200 W. It is meant for saving work and shutting down, not for long power cuts.

### Is 600 VA enough for a desktop computer?
A 600 VA UPS on this list supports up to 360 W. That covers a typical office desktop with one monitor. A gaming PC or a setup with two monitors may need a 1,000 VA / 600 W unit like the ZEBRONICS U1205.

### Can I connect a printer to a UPS?
Not a laser printer. APC does not recommend connecting laser printers to its Back-UPS products because their high power draw can drain the battery. Plug a laser printer directly into the wall.

### Can a computer UPS run my Wi-Fi router?
Yes, but some models cut off at very light loads. The ZEBRONICS Zeb-U725 shuts down in battery mode below 40 W. If you only need the router, a dedicated router UPS is cheaper and lasts much longer.
