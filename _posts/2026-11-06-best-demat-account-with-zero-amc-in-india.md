If you buy a few stocks or funds a year, a yearly demat fee can quietly become the biggest cost of investing. The annual or account maintenance charge (AMC) is what a broker bills you just to keep your demat account open, whether you trade or not.

This guide shows which popular brokers charge nothing, which ones waive the fee only in year one, and how the Basic Services Demat Account (BSDA) can cut the bill if you hold under a set amount.

## Quick answer

- **Zero AMC for life, regular account:** Groww and Paytm Money list a Rs 0 AMC on a regular individual demat account.
- **Free only in year one:** Zerodha, Upstox and Angel One waive AMC for the first year, then charge for non-BSDA accounts.
- **Small portfolio on a paid broker:** Ask for a BSDA, which has no AMC when holdings are up to Rs 4 lakh at brokers such as Zerodha and Angel One.

| --- | --- |

**Best for:** active or long-term stock investors who like Zerodha's tools and are fine with a small fee later, especially while holdings stay under Rs 4 lakh.

## Angel One: quarterly billing after year one

Angel One's own help page says AMC is zero in the first year. From year two, non-BSDA clients pay Rs 60 + GST every quarter, charged after the first trade each quarter. For BSDA clients, AMC is zero when holdings are under Rs 4 lakh, and Rs 100 + GST a year between Rs 4 lakh and Rs 10 lakh.

## How to pick in three steps

### 1. Check how big your portfolio is

Under Rs 4 lakh, a BSDA can make AMC zero even at brokers that charge for regular accounts. Above Rs 10 lakh, BSDA is no longer available at Zerodha.

### 2. Count your sells

DP charges apply on every sale. A zero-AMC broker can still cost more than a paid-AMC broker if you sell often and its DP charge is higher.

### 3. Think about a second account

Opening is free at most brokers, and you can hold accounts with several brokers under one PAN. Some investors keep a zero-AMC account for long-term holdings and another for trading.

## FAQ

### Which demat account has zero AMC?
Groww and Paytm Money list Rs 0 AMC on a regular individual demat account, per a September 2026 comparison of broker pricing pages.

### Is the first year really free at Zerodha, Upstox and Angel One?
Yes, all three waive AMC in year one for new accounts. Zerodha says its waiver covers resident individual accounts opened on or after 1 June 2026, and only the primary demat account.

### What is a BSDA?
A Basic Services Demat Account is a lower-cost demat for small investors. At Zerodha and Angel One, AMC is zero when holdings are up to Rs 4 lakh.

### Does zero AMC mean a free demat account?
No. Brokerage, DP charges on sales, and taxes still apply.

### Can I hold demat accounts with more than one broker?
Yes, you can hold demat accounts with several brokers under the same PAN.
