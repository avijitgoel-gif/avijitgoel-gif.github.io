## Home safe or bank locker?

A home safe is not a replacement for a bank locker, and a bank locker is not as fully protected as many people think.

Under the RBI's master circular on safe deposit lockers, if the contents are lost because of fire, theft, burglary, dacoity, robbery, building collapse or fraud by bank employees, the bank's liability is **100 times the prevailing annual locker rent**. Banks are not liable for natural calamities such as earthquakes or floods, or for the customer's own negligence. The same circular lets a bank ask for a term deposit covering three years' rent plus break-open charges when it allots a locker.

That cap can be far below the value of the gold inside. A November 2026 Business Standard explainer adds practical advice: banks do not keep a record of what is in your locker, so keep your own list and photos outside it, and consider insurance if the contents are valuable.

**A sensible split for most families:**

- **Bank locker:** wedding gold, original property deeds, anything you rarely touch
- **Home safe:** passports, everyday jewellery, cash for emergencies, cheque books, spare keys, hard drives with family photos

## How to choose a home safe

### Weight and mounting matter more than the keypad

A safe that can be lifted can be stolen. Light safes such as the 5 to 5.5 kg models here should always be bolted to a wall or the floor of a steel cupboard, using the hardware supplied. Heavier safes like the 16 to 17 kg models are harder to carry off, but they still should be bolted.

### Look at door steel and bolts

The door takes the prying attack. The Amazon Basics 8-gauge door and the Yale's 16 mm bolts are the clearest specs published here. Listings that give no steel thickness at all deserve more caution.

### Plan for dead batteries

Every keypad safe here runs on batteries. Make sure the backup key is stored somewhere other than inside the safe. The Godrej adds USB emergency power, which helps if the key goes missing too.

### Fingerprint is about convenience, not strength

A fingerprint reader makes access quicker, but it does not make the steel any thicker. Pay for biometrics only if several people use the safe daily.

### Check the size you actually need

Measure the cupboard shelf before you buy. A 41 to 58 litre safe will not fit in many almirahs, and the 10 litre Godrej will not take an A4 file box.

## FAQ

### Which is the best digital safe locker for home under ₹10,000?
The Amazon Basics 58L at about ₹6,309 on 11 November 2026. It weighs 17 kg, has an 8-gauge steel door, two live bolts and 4.0 stars from 31,319 Amazon ratings.

### Is a home safe better than a bank locker?
Neither is complete on its own. Under RBI rules a bank's liability for theft, fire or employee fraud is capped at 100 times the annual locker rent, while a home safe gives daily access but only stops casual theft. Many families keep rarely used gold in a bank locker and everyday valuables in a home safe.

### What happens if the battery of a digital safe dies?
All five safes here come with a backup key. The Godrej Forte Pro also accepts USB emergency power. Keep the backup key outside the safe.

### Should I bolt my home safe to the wall?
Yes. Every safe in this guide comes with mounting holes and hardware. A light safe that is not bolted down can simply be carried away.

### Is a fingerprint safe more secure than a keypad safe?
Not by itself. A fingerprint reader makes access faster and avoids sharing a PIN, but the steel, bolts and mounting decide how hard the safe is to break into.
