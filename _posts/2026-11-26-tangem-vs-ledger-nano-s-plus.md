## Where each one wins

**The screen.** A Ledger shows the receiving address and amount on its own display, and you approve with its buttons. If malware on your computer swaps the address, you can catch it on the device. Tangem has no display, so you rely on your phone's screen.

**Phones.** Ledger says the Nano S Plus does not work with iOS. Tangem works by tapping the card on any NFC phone, iPhone included.

**Backup.** Ledger's backup is a recovery phrase you write on the three sheets in the box. Lose the phrase and the device together and the coins are gone. Tangem's backup is the second card, so there is nothing to write down. Keep the cards at different addresses, or pay ₹735 more for the 3-card pack.

**Warranty.** Tangem's listing promises 25 years. The Ledger purple listing says 1 year.
