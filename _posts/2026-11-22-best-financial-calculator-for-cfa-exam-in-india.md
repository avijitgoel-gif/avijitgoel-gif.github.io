## What CFA Institute and GARP actually allow

CFA Institute's policy names exactly two families:

1. Texas Instruments BA II Plus, including the BA II Plus Professional
2. Hewlett Packard 12C, including the HP 12C Platinum, 12C Platinum 25th anniversary edition, 12C 30th anniversary edition and HP 12C Prestige

You must bring your own. You cannot borrow one at the test centre or share with another candidate. Covers, keystroke cards and loose batteries are allowed in the room; instruction manuals are not. You may also keep a small screwdriver to change the battery in a BA II Plus ([CFA Institute](https://www.cfainstitute.org/about/governance/policies/cfa-calculator-policy)).

GARP's list for the FRM exam is a little wider. It allows the same HP 12C family and BA II Plus family, plus the HP 10B II, HP 10BII+ and HP 20B ([GARP FRM exam policies](https://www.garp.org/frm/exam-policies)). If you plan to do both CFA and FRM, a BA II Plus covers both.

The Casio FC-200V and every scientific calculator, including the popular Casio fx-991 series, are not on either list.

CFA Institute's own calculator guide calls it "the most popular calculator among CFA candidates due to its intuitive interface and lower learning curve" ([CFA Institute guide, PDF](https://www.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/docs/programs/cfa-program/candidate-resources/mastering-your-calculator-for-the-cfa-exam.pdf)).

**Who should buy it:** CFA Level I to III and FRM candidates who want the cheapest approved option with the largest base of tutorials and keystroke guides.

### 2. TI BA II Plus Professional: best if you want the extra functions

Texas Instruments says the Professional is nearly identical to the standard model except for a few extra variables: net future value (NFV), payback (PB), discounted payback (DPB), reinvestment rate and modified internal rate of return (MIRR) in the cash flow worksheet, and modified duration in the bond worksheet ([TI knowledge base 18069](https://education.ti.com/en/customer-support/knowledge-base/financial-calculators/general-information/18069)). It also stores 32 uneven cash flows instead of 24 and has six depreciation methods instead of four ([TI BA II Plus Professional](https://education.ti.com/en/products/calculators/financial/baii-plus-professional)).

On Amazon.in it had 4.4 stars from 5,818 ratings, "200+ bought in past month", ranked #3 in Financial & Business, and listed a 3 year warranty, at about ₹5,650 ([Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0001EMLZW?tag=rupeesmart01-21){:rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"}).

A small practical difference: TI's Professional guidebook says the back cover comes off after removing one screw, while the standard BA II Plus manual describes four screws plus a metal battery cover. Both use a CR2032.

**Watch out for:** you pay about ₹1,400 more for functions you can often work around. For example, TI notes that NFV is not a built-in key on the standard BA II Plus, but you can get it through the TVM worksheet after computing NPV ([TI knowledge base 25790](https://education.ti.com/en/customer-support/knowledge-base/financial-calculators/product-usage/25790)). CFA Institute's guide does say the Professional has better build quality.

**Who should buy it:** candidates who will keep using the calculator at work in project finance, credit or fixed income, and Level II or III candidates who want fewer keystrokes.

## Practise on it: one Indian example

Learning the keystrokes matters more than the model. Here is a home loan EMI on the BA II Plus for ₹30 lakh at 8.5% a year for 20 years:

1. Press 2nd, then P/Y, key in 12 and press ENTER. Press 2nd, then QUIT.
2. Key in 240 and press N.
3. Key in 8.5 and press I/Y.
4. Key in 3000000 and press PV. Key in 0 and press FV.
5. Press CPT, then PMT. The display shows about -26,034.70, so the EMI is about ₹26,035 a month.

Over 240 months that adds up to about ₹32.48 lakh of interest on a ₹30 lakh loan. Remember to set P/Y back to 1 for annual CFA problems. TI's guidebook lists 1 as the default for P/Y and C/Y after a full reset, and the calculator shows two decimal places in its examples. CFA Institute's guide suggests setting four decimal places for more precise answers.

## How to choose: a quick checklist

1. **Exam first.** CFA: BA II Plus or HP 12C families only. FRM: the same, plus HP 10B II, 10BII+ and 20B.
2. **Standard or Professional.** The standard BA II Plus is enough for the exam. Pay for the Professional only if you want NFV, MIRR, payback and modified duration as built-in keys.
3. **Practise on the exact unit.** Buy it at least two to three months before the exam so the keystrokes become automatic.
4. **Battery.** CR2032 for both TI models, whatever the listing table says.
5. **Reset before the exam.** CFA Institute's guide says to clear memory before the exam starts.

## FAQ

### Which calculator is allowed in the CFA exam?
Only the Texas Instruments BA II Plus (including the BA II Plus Professional) and the HP 12C (including the 12C Platinum, Platinum 25th anniversary, 30th anniversary and 12C Prestige), as per CFA Institute's policy updated 12 January 2026. Using or carrying any other calculator voids your result.

### Is the BA II Plus Professional worth it over the standard BA II Plus?
For passing the CFA exam, no. Texas Instruments says the two are nearly identical except for NFV, payback, discounted payback, MIRR and modified duration. On 22 November 2026 the Professional cost about Rs 5,650 against Rs 4,250 for the standard model on Amazon.in.

### Can I use the Casio FC-200V or fx-991 in the CFA or FRM exam?
No. Neither appears on CFA Institute's or GARP's approved lists. The Casio FC-200V is a good financial calculator for MBA or office work, but not for these exams.

### Which battery does the BA II Plus use?
A CR2032 lithium coin cell, according to Texas Instruments. Some Amazon.in listings say CR123A or LR44, which do not match. Replacing the battery resets the calculator, and CFA Institute allows loose batteries in the exam room.

### Which calculators are allowed in the FRM exam?
GARP allows the HP 12C family, the TI BA II Plus (including Professional), the HP 10B II, the HP 10BII+ and the HP 20B.
