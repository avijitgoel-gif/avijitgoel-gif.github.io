## The renewal jump, explained

Intro prices are discounts for new customers. When your term ends, the plan renews at the standard rate:

- Single: ₹69 to ₹289/mo, about 4.2 times the intro price
- Premium: about 3 times
- Unlimited and Cloud Startup: about 2.6 times

HostAdvice points out that Single, the cheapest-looking plan, has the steepest renewal multiple of the four ([HostAdvice India pricing](https://gb.hostadvice.com/hosting-company/hostinger-reviews/hostinger-india-pricing/)).

### How to soften renewal costs

1. **Pick the longest term you are comfortable with.** The ₹69, ₹139 and ₹199 prices are for 48 months. Longer terms lock the low rate for longer.
2. **Set a calendar reminder 30 days before renewal.** Check if a new promotion is cheaper than renewing at the standard rate.
3. **Do not overbuy.** If you only need one site, Single is fine. But if you plan a second site within a year, Premium avoids an upgrade later.

## Which plan should you choose?

### Single: one basic site

Good for a personal page or a simple portfolio. You get 1 website, 10 GB SSD storage, a free domain, weekly backups and a free mailbox. The limit is one site, which is restrictive if you want to test a second project.

### Premium: the sweet spot for bloggers

Premium hosts up to 3 websites, has 20 GB SSD storage, a domain free for 1 year and 2 mailboxes per website free for 1 year. For a new blog or affiliate site, this gives room for a second site without moving to a bigger plan.

The headline prices on Hostinger's India page are for the 48-month term.
